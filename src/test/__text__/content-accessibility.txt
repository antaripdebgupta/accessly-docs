Accessibility PrinciplesOur foundation for building inclusive, standard-conforming software for all users.Four Pillars of Accessly UI1. Keyboard Operability (WCAG 2.1)Every single component is operable without a mouse. Standard keystrokes (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, Arrow keys, Escape) follow established W3C APG patterns.2. Focus Management & Restoration (WCAG 2.4.3)Modal overlays trap focus within active boundaries. When overlays close, focus returns cleanly to the triggering element. Dynamic widgets utilize roving tabindex to preserve a single tab stop.3. Semantic ARIA & Live Regions (WCAG 4.1.2 & 4.1.3)Components ship explicit ARIA attributes (roles, states, expanded/selected/current flags). Asynchronous operations announce updates using polite or assertive live regions.4. Contrast & Motion Preferences (WCAG 1.4.3 & 2.2.2)All text maintains at least 4.5:1 contrast against warm canvas backgrounds. Interactive control borders exceed 3:1. Animations automatically disable under prefers-reduced-motion.Visible Focus Ring GuaranteesFocus indicators are never hidden or suppressed.
